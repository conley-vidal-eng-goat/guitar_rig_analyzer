PROCEDURAL DEVIATIONS — RECORDED, NOT SMOOTHED OVER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WINDOW WAS 137.785 s, NOT THE FIXED 120 s.
   Cause: operator-side (agent) tool-call latency. The passive serial read that
   was holding the window open returned early at 75 s on a 32 KiB buffer cap,
   and the gap before resuming added ~18 s. 'T' was sent as soon as the overrun
   was noticed.
   Effect: +14.8 % over the fixed window, which EXCEEDS the 10 % tolerance
   pi/tools/t1_diff.py applies. Runs 1/3/4 must use the same 120 s target, so
   this run is NOT window-matched to a future control and would be flagged.
   This alone is grounds to re-run run 2 once the harness defect is fixed.

2. THE BOOT BANNER WAS NOT CAPTURED.
   The port was attached after the banner had scrolled. Accepted rather than
   voided because all three identity items it carries were obtained from
   equivalent sources actually captured here: the build stamp from every 5 s
   block (printBuildIdentity() has two callers), display_target from
   '[T1] display_target=Pi 5 (UART)', and the session id from '[Link] Session
   id: 9'. docs/T1_run2_procedure.md §8 lists a missed banner as "void and
   retry"; that condition is over-strict and should be amended.

3. THE PI CAPTURE DID NOT SPAN THE WHOLE WINDOW.
   Capture 06:31:09Z-06:33:39Z; window ~06:31:50Z-06:34:08Z. The final ~29 s of
   the window had no Pi-side reader. Consequence of deviation 1.
   Materially harmless, and provably so: there is NO hardware flow control on
   this link (Serial1, no attachCts/attachRts anywhere, RTS/CTS not wired), so
   the Teensy transmits at baud rate regardless of whether the Pi drains and
   CANNOT observe the difference. No [T1] metric can depend on it.
   It is still a deviation from "reader consuming normally for the whole
   window" and is recorded as one.

4. Ordering: the Pi capture was started BEFORE the serial precondition checks,
   so the checks ran inside the settle period. Deliberate, to protect the
   150 s capture's coverage of the window.
